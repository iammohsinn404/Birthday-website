# About the Project and the Working

### PAGES ORDER:

**Intro Page → Puzzle Page → Monkey Page → Cake Page → Prank Page → Birthday Page → Scratch Page! ENDED!!!**

---

## INTRO PAGE:

First, there is an **Intro Page**, where you can see a **Yes/No** option, profile, music, Night/Day Mode, and Levels — you can jump to any level too.

There is also a **Create Link** option in the profile. When you click it, it will ask you some information about the person: **name, date, month, year of birthday, and the relation**.

Then click **Generate Link**. When you copy and send it to another person, they **can’t see any Create Link option**. They will only see the birthday page personalized for him/her, like the name, and the relation will come exactly as you wrote it.

---

## OTHER PAGES EXCEPT INTRO PAGE:

When you click the **profile**, you can see three options:

- **Restart** — goes back to the Intro.
- **Refresh** — restarts the page you are currently on.
- **Night/Day Mode** — where you can change the themes.

And the **cursor will only be visible in Night Mode**, inspired by **Macondo!**

The same **music button** is also available.

---

## PUZZLE PAGE:

Second, there is a **Puzzle Page**.

You have to **match the cards** and Pass.

Once you pass, you can continue to the next page.

---

## MONKEY PAGE:

After passing, you will click the button, and you will come to the **Monkey Page**.

You will see a **gift**, and you have to click it for a surprise.

When you click it, you will see the monkey, and it will be **you — just kidding 😂**

---

## CAKE PAGE:

There will be a **Cake** that we have to eat piece by piece by clicking on them.

When we click the pieces, it will play a **weird Burp Sound**. 😂

Then click the **Next** button.

---

## PRANK PAGE:

You just have to click the button, and you will see a **surprise with a poop and a sound**.

Hehe 🤣

Click the **Next** button.

---

## BIRTHDAY PAGE:

You will see a **loading screen at first**, and then the Birthday Page will appear.

You can click the **Surprise button** at the start or at the end for a **hidden letter**.

The **Create Link** also works here. The text will change to:

**Happy Birthday, Mohsin! 🎂🎉**

---

## SCRATCH PAGE:

A simple **Scratch Page** where you have to find the surprise by scratching the place.

Once you find it, the message will appear.

**Thank you 😊**
